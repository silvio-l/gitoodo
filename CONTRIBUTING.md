# Contributing to Gitoodo

Thanks for your interest. Gitoodo has not been released yet: the source code, the issue tracker
and the full contribution guide arrive in this repository with the first release.

Until then:

- **Ideas, questions and feedback:** write to us through the
  [contact form on the website](https://silvio-l.github.io/gitoodo/#download). Your message reaches
  us by email and is not published.
- **Security problems:** report them privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Everyone taking part follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). From the first release on,
contributions are released under the [MIT licence](LICENSE).
